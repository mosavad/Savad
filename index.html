About

I make ideas visible.

I’m Mohammed Savad, a creative individual based in Dubai, with interests in graphic design, illustration, video editing and visual storytelling. I enjoy transforming ideas into compelling visuals, exploring different creative approaches through news graphics, posters, illustrations and digital media.

My academic background in B.A. Programme (History and Political Science) at the University of Delhi has shaped the way I think, observe and interpret the world. It has broadened my perspective on people, ideas and the world around us, complementing my curiosity and approach to visual communication.

I served as the Media & Publicity Representative on the Executive Committee of SARGA (Delhi University South Malayalees Association), contributing to promotional campaigns, creative initiatives and visual communication. I also served as the Chief Editor of Quarantined, SARGA’s magazine, gaining experience in editorial planning, content development and publication design. These experiences strengthened my skills in media, communication, design and storytelling.

My creative interests extend beyond graphic design. From Arabic calligraphy and Malayalam poetry to editorial layouts, illustrations and video editing, I find inspiration in diverse forms of art and expression. I believe every creative work carries a story, and the way it is communicated makes all the difference.

Currently based in Dubai, I’m open to freelance projects, creative collaborations and opportunities in graphic design, illustration and video editing.

Malayalam poem

A Malayalam free-verse poem that explores the growing influence of technology on everyday human life. Through the routine of a day, from waking up to going to bed, it portrays how digital platforms, automated tasks, and virtual interactions have become an integral part of modern living. The poem highlights the irony of people remaining physically close to their loved ones while increasingly communicating through screens, gradually turning life into a mechanical cycle of repetitive activities. It reflects on the unnoticed transformation of humanity, where the convenience of technology begins to shape not only daily habits but also human relationships and the way we experience life.

Malayalam poem

A Malayalam free-verse poem exploring oppression, violence, and the human cost of standing against injustice. Through dark imagery and symbolic references to a spider’s web, bloodstained walls, and selfless sacrifice, it reflects on the lives lost in the face of violence, the silencing of dissenting voices, and the fading traces of their struggles with the passage of time.
